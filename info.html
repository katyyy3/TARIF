TARIF.

Tarif is a typeface family inspired by the multicultural
vutopia of Convivencia — the peaceful coexistence of Muslims,
Christians and Jews in tenth century Andalusia, a region which
played an important role in bringing to Europe the classics of
Greek philosophy, together with Islamic culture and aesthetics.
Designed for Zetafonts by Andrea Tartarelli, Tarif is a slab
serif typeface with a humanist skeleton and inverted contrast,
subtly mixing latin zeist, calligraphic details, extreme
ink-traps, and postmodern unorthodox reinvention of traditional
grotesque letter shapes.

The design of Tarif, perfect for titling, logo and display use,
is complemented by a wide range of seven weights allowing for
solid editorial use and great readability in body text. Matching
italics have been designed with the help of Cosimo Lorenzo
Pancini, while Rania Azmi has collaborated on the design of
the arabic version of Tarif, where the humanist shapes and
inverted contrast of the latin letters find a natural connection
with modern arabic letterforms.

As buoyant as reliable, Tarif includes also a wide array of Open Type Features (alternates, ligatures, positional numerals, case sensitive punctuation) to make design smooth and multi-script projects as exciting as a surf ride in the sunny Tarifa.

TARIF.

Tarif is one of those fonts that instantly gives your design
a really unique personality. It mixes a slab-serif structure
with softer, humanist details, so it feels both bold and
friendly at the same time. Because it has those inverted
contrasts and slightly quirky shapes, it brings this cool
mix of history and modern creativity—like something inspired
by old manuscripts but redesigned for a contemporary audience.
When you use Tarif, the overall vibe is artistic, approachable,
and a little bit unconventional in a good way.

It works great when you want your project to look thoughtful
and expressive without feeling too formal or stiff. As a student
designer, it’s a fun typeface to experiment with because it
stands out naturally and adds a sense of personality to whatever
you’re making, whether that’s branding, posters, or more
editorial-style layouts. If you want something that feels cultured
but still fresh and visually interesting, Tarif is a really strong choice.

ZetaFonts
Zeta Fonts is an award-winning type foundry that started in 2001 in Florence, created by Francesco Canovaro, Debora Manetti, and Cosimo Lorenzo Pancini. They have a really wide range of modern type families and updated revivals, and their team includes designers with a lot of experience in branding and typography. Zeta Fonts also works with big brands to develop custom type solutions, and they’re involved in promoting multi-script type culture through things like TypeCampus, conferences, and type design courses.
Andrea Taratarelli

Andrea Tartarelli first studied at the Academy of Fine Arts of Carrara and actually started out working as a marble sculptor. He eventually discovered graphic design and typography, hoping it might be a little easier than carving stone — but he quickly realized type design can be just as intense in its own way. He kept diving deeper into typography, studying at the Plantin Institute in Antwerp, and now teaches Type Design at IED Florence. Over the years, he’s designed several well-known typefaces like Tarif, Malik, Marcovaldo, and Calvino, all of which have earned recognition and awards. Andrea has also collaborated on many other Zetafonts projects, including Blacker, Monterchi, and Stinger, which have also won multiple typography awards. He currently lives and works in Tuscany, balancing teaching, type design, and his lifelong love for creativity.

Cosimo Lorenzo Pancini

Cosimo Lorenzo Pancini has been obsessed with letters since he was nine, when he bought his first typeface — an Arnold Boecklin letraset sheet. From that moment on, he never really stopped experimenting with typography, whether through drawing, design, or teaching. He works as a type designer, visual arts educator, and creative director across print, digital, and video. Cosimo co-founded the Zetafonts type foundry with Francesco Canovaro and Debora Manetti, and has since designed more than fifty type families. Some of his standout projects include Cocogoose, Blacker (chosen by MyFonts as one of the best new families of 2019), Monterchi (CA Typography Award 2020, MyFonts Hidden Gem 2019), Stinger (CA Typography Award 2021), Stadio Now (TDC68), and Keratine (CA Typography Award 2023). He also has a cat named Bodoni and owns way too many books — which honestly feels pretty on-brand for a type designer.

Francesco Canovaro

Francesco Canovaro got into visual design and typography after studying both painting and computer programming, which sounds like a random combo but actually fits perfectly with what he does now. He started the branding agency Kmzero and the Zetafonts type foundry with Debora Manetti and Cosimo Lorenzo Pancini, and since then he’s created more than forty type families, plus tons of custom fonts for different projects. Some of his best-known typefaces are Aquawax, Heading (a big MyFonts bestseller), Blacker, Kitsch, Stadio Now, and Bakemono, all of which have picked up awards or been highlighted by major font platforms. Students who’ve taken his creative direction or typography classes say he’s equal parts intense and inspiring. At type conferences, he’s the guy listening to a talk while designing a whole new font on his laptop. He lives in Florence with his wife and two kids — and apparently all their names end with consonants, which he seems oddly proud of.
