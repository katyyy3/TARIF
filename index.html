Slab Serif Font
TARIF.
STYLE.

Tarif is one of those typefaces that immediately gives your design
a strong sense of personality. It blends a slab-serif structure with
softer, more humanist features, so it manages to feel bold while still
staying warm and approachable. The inverted contrast and slightly
unconventional shapes add this subtle quirkiness that makes it
feel different in a good way—almost like it’s carrying pieces of
historical calligraphy but expressed through a clean, contemporary lens.

What makes Tarif really interesting is that it doesn’t fall neatly into
one category. It’s not overly traditional, but it’s not purely modern either.
It sits in this in-between space where it feels thoughtful, expressive,
and somewhat artistic. Those unique letterforms create an instant sense of
character, which makes the font memorable even when used in simple layouts.
It has this quiet confidence—noticeable without trying too hard.

When you use Tarif, the overall vibe becomes more refined and intentional.
Projects gain a kind of “curated” look, like every choice was made with purpose.
At the same time, there’s nothing pretentious or stiff about it. Because of
its mix of strong structure and humanist details, it comes across as friendly
and inviting. It’s the kind of typeface that can communicate professionalism
while still feeling creative and open-minded.

"Type Without Frontiers."


As a student designer, Tarif is especially fun to experiment with because it
responds so well to different contexts. In branding, it can give a logo or
wordmark a distinctive voice without overwhelming the visual system. In posters,
it brings energy and style that make the layout feel more dynamic and intentional.
For editorial work, the family’s range of weights makes it easy to balance hierarchy
while maintaining a cohesive look.

Another big advantage is how adaptable it is. Tarif can feel historical when paired
with classic imagery or color palettes, but it can also look surprisingly modern when
used with clean, minimal layouts. Its versatility makes it a great tool when you’re
still developing your own design style and want something that naturally elevates your
work.

Overall, Tarif strikes that rare balance between cultured and contemporary—it feels
artistic, fresh, and visually engaging all at once. If you want a typeface with
personality that still maintains clarity and structure, Tarif is genuinely one of the
strongest choices you can make.
